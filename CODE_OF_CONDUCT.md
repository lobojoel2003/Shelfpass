# Code of Conduct

Shelfpass exists to help incarcerated people read. We expect everyone who takes part in the project to act in that spirit.

## Our standards

- Be kind and assume good faith. Volunteers come with very different technical backgrounds.
- Respect the dignity and privacy of incarcerated people. Never share their names, ID
  numbers, letters or photos in project spaces.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and
  sexualised language are not acceptable.
- Credit others' work, and be honest about what you know and what you've verified,
  especially when adding facility rules.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or a private security advisory. Maintainers may remove content and block participants who
break this code. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
